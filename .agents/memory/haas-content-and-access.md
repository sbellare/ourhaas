name: HAAS content and access
description: Product intent and source-content rules for the HAAS community website.
---

The user wants the contents of ourhaas.com reworked for ourhaas.org, grounded in Mangalorean Konkani culture and Konkani roots in the USA.

**Why:** This is a community-site migration, not a new organization or invented brand history.

**How to apply:** Retain original membership policies, community resources, and source document links. Do not invent event dates or present historical events as upcoming merely to populate the homepage.

The user selected one administrator account. Administrator access must require verification of the owner-assigned email address, in development and production, rather than granting access to the first person who signs up.

**Why:** Event editing is restricted to administrators, and development and production have separate identities.

**How to apply:** Preserve server-enforced authorization and a calendar date picker for event editing. Never add public self-promotion or a preview authentication bypass.
